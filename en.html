Responsible destruction

We don't just dispose of it. We destroy it responsibly and securely.

Handing your equipment to us isn't throwing it in the trash. It's a process with two parts: the physical destruction of the equipment, and the secure wiping of the information it holds — with environmentally responsible handling at every step.

🔒

Secure data wiping

Before any physical destruction, the information on each device is securely erased — so your company can hand it over with peace of mind, with no risk of sensitive data being exposed.

♻️

Responsible final disposal

Equipment is processed with environmentally responsible handling, not improvised disposal. We are in the process of obtaining official certification for responsible final disposal, to back this up formally.

About us

Corporate technology is what we know how to do.

Ratazzi México comes from a real track record in corporate technology: since 1993, we have managed and leased computer equipment for some of the world's biggest brands — Volkswagen, Audi, and Daimler, across multi-year contracts covering more than 5,000 units.

Today we apply that same experience to the responsible destruction of corporate computer equipment: we collect it directly from companies, and we make sure its final destination — and that of the information it holds — is handled securely and responsibly.
